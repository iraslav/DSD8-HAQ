## Bring-up order actually followed (spec §8.4 / guide slide 56)

T05 (NOP) → T01 (LDI) → T23 (JMP) → T15–T22 (ALU) → T09–T14 (LD/ST) →
T24–T30 (Bcc) → T31–T35 (I/O) → T36–T40 (programs).

## Items that need the team (cannot be done by tooling alone)

- Real member names / GitHub usernames in `doc/m1/team_and_ownership.md` and `.github/CODEOWNERS`.
- The partner team's review comments (`doc/design_review.md` has the checklist and our self-review).
- Each member's oral defence (10 min, no notes) — `doc/defence_prep.md` is the study guide.
- When the instructor's official T01–T40 suite is distributed, drop it into `tests/conformance/`
  (our own T01–T40 are written from the §8.4 descriptions and are kept under the same names).
